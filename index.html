SALU Community

Student guidance, all in one place.

An independent, student-run platform supporting applicants and students of Shah Abdul Latif University, Khairpur. It brings admission information, university resources and interactive preparation tools together in one accessible website.

28Departments & institutes
7Faculties covered

What’s inside the website

  • Admissions & merit listsApplication guidance, schedules, announcements and merit-list downloads.
  • Departments, seats & feesProgram information, seat allocations, fee structures and scholarship guidance.
  • Aggregate calculatorCalculate CPN using test, intermediate and matriculation marks.
  • Past papers & test preparationSolved papers, downloadable PDFs, online quizzes and free mock tests.
  • Community & supportStudent FAQs, contact options and department representative registration.
