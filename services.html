FAQ

Common questions

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the pages and features you need. Send us a message on WhatsApp with your idea and we will share a clear quote before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

A simple website is usually ready within a few days to two weeks. Larger apps take longer, and we give you a timeline up front.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every site we build is mobile-first and tested on small phone screens, because most visitors use phones.

Can I update the website myself later?

Yes. We build it so common content can be changed easily, or we use WordPress, and we show you how.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We help with updates, fixes and improvements after the site goes live.
